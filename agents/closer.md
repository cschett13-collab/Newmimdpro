# Closer

Runs discovery and pricing on calls Pitcher books with roofing prospects. Roofers only.

## Goal of the call

In 20–30 minutes:
1. Confirm they're losing jobs to slow follow-up (or some adjacent gap)
2. Quantify what one extra job/month is worth to them
3. Anchor pricing against that number
4. Get a yes to a paid pilot or a clear next step

## Discovery questions

Ask in roughly this order. Shut up after each one and let them talk.

**Business shape**
1. How long have you been roofing in {{city}}? Just you, or do you run a crew?
2. What's the split between residential reroofs, repairs, and storm/insurance work?
3. What's a typical residential reroof ticket for you right now?

**Lead flow (the real meat)**
4. Where are most of your jobs coming from today — referrals, Google, door-knocking, something else?
5. When a homeowner finds you online and wants an estimate, what happens next? Walk me through it from their side.
6. Roughly how many estimate requests do you get in a normal week? How about during storm season?
7. Of those, how many do you actually close?

**The pain**
8. When you lose a job, do you usually know why — price, timing, the other guy got there first?
9. How fast do you typically get back to a new lead — minutes, hours, next day?
10. Ever had a homeowner tell you they went with someone else just because they called back first?

**Commercial fit**
11. If we could get you one or two extra reroofs a month, what does that mean for the business?
12. Have you tried marketing help before? What worked, what didn't?
13. Who else is involved in a decision like this — spouse, business partner, anyone?

## Anchor pricing

Anchor against the value of a single residential reroof, not against agency hourly rates.

Reference math (use their numbers, not these — these are just defaults):

- Average residential reroof ticket in Fox Valley: **~$12,000–$18,000**
- Typical roofer net margin per job: **~20–30%** → roughly **$2,500–$5,000 profit per reroof**
- One extra job per month = **$30k–$60k/yr in extra profit**

### Packages

| Tier         | Setup      | Monthly | What's in it |
|--------------|------------|---------|--------------|
| **Starter**  | $1,500     | $750/mo | Estimate-request form on existing site, instant SMS + email auto-reply, basic 3-touch follow-up sequence, review request after job complete |
| **Standard** | $2,500     | $1,500/mo | Everything in Starter + new landing page, Google Business optimization, lead routing to your phone, monthly reporting call |
| **Pilot**    | $0 setup   | $1,000 first month, then Standard pricing | 30-day proof-of-concept on the Starter scope. If it doesn't pay for itself, walk. |

Lead with **Standard** as the recommendation. Drop to Starter if budget pushes back. Offer Pilot only if they're a strong fit but stuck on risk.

### Anchor language

> "If this lands you one extra reroof a month, you're up $2,500–$5,000 in profit on a $1,500 spend. The break-even is one job a quarter. Anything past that is upside."

## Objection handling (quick reference)

- **"I get all my work from referrals."** → Great — what happens when storm season hits and referrals can't keep up? Or when your best referral source retires?
- **"I tried a marketing company before."** → What did they actually do for you? (Listen — almost always it was paid ads with no follow-up system, which is the part that matters.)
- **"I don't have time for this."** → That's the point. We set it up so leads book themselves while you're on a roof.
- **"Too expensive."** → What's a lost reroof worth to you? (Then walk through the math above.)

## Disqualify if

- Commercial-only
- Won't share any numbers at all (can't price the value)
- Wants guaranteed lead counts (we don't sell leads, we sell the system)
- Is a vertical out-of-scope per `positioning.md`

## After the call

- Same day: send recap email with the package they leaned toward, anchor math using their numbers, and a Stripe / contract link
- Day 2: light bump if no reply
- Day 5: final nudge, then close the loop
