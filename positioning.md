# Positioning

## Primary ICP

**Local roofing companies in the Fox Valley area, Wisconsin.**

Geo focus:
- Appleton
- Neenah
- Menasha
- Kaukauna
- Oshkosh
- Green Bay

Company shape we target:
- Owner-operator or small crew (1–5 trucks)
- Residential reroofs, repairs, storm/insurance work
- Doing real revenue but losing jobs to faster-responding competitors
- Marketing is mostly word-of-mouth + a tired website + maybe Google profile

Why now: spring/storm season drives a spike in homeowner search intent. Roofers who can't capture and follow up on leads quickly bleed jobs to whoever calls back first.

## What we sell them

Lead capture + speed-to-lead follow-up so they stop losing jobs to the competitor who answered the phone first. Concretely: estimate request form on the site, instant text/email response, simple follow-up sequence, review request after the job.

## Out of scope (for now)

The following verticals are **not** primary targets and should not be prospected, pitched, or referenced in copy:

- Chiropractors
- HVAC
- Legal / law firms

If a great-fit lead in one of these verticals comes in inbound, route to Leanna — do not pursue outbound.

## Secondary track — Dental (Leanna only)

Dental remains a live track but is owned and run by **Leanna** separately from the main agent loop.

**Agent rule:** Scout, Pitcher, and Closer ignore dental entirely unless Leanna explicitly asks for help on a specific dental task. Do not include dental prospects in lists, do not write dental copy, do not blend dental messaging into roofing campaigns.
